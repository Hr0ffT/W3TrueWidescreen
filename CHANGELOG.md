# W3TrueWidescreen changelog

- **1.19** — `StretchedConsole`: the in-game console and the top bar can be stretched over the screen width as in the original game, while the rest of the interface stays unstretched. Windowed mode (`-window`): the console's see-through gaps are black again instead of showing the world.
- **1.18** — Hero portraits and the cutscene letterbox can be placed in a centred area such as 16:9 on very wide screens: `HeroBarAspect` and `CinematicBarsAspect` replace `HeroBarEdge` and `CinematicFullWidth` (empty = the screen edges as before, `4:3` = the old 0). `CinematicAspect` is renamed to `CinematicDialogueAspect`, the dialogue stays inside the letterbox.
- **1.17** — `CinematicAspect`: on very wide screens the cutscene speaker's portrait and text can stay in a centred area such as 16:9, with the letterbox still across the whole width. The contents of the LAN, saved game, custom campaign and replay screens and of the game lobby are now centred in their widened panels, like on the custom game screen. The occupancy marks above burrows and gold mines are no longer drawn over the console. Custom loading pictures of Battle.net servers (Eurobattle.net): the minimap fits the picture's frame and the loading bar sits in its slot.
- **1.16** — `MenuAspect`: on very wide screens (three monitors) the menus can stay in a centred area such as 16:9, with the 3D backgrounds still over the whole screen. `MenuBackgroundFull=0` now works: the 3D menu backgrounds are stretched as in the original game. Text shadows no longer slide to the right on wide screens. Score screen: sharp on very wide screens, without the lighter 4:3 patch in the middle (past about 21:9 the sheet is extended rather than stretched). 1.28.5: the camera frame on the minimap can be dragged to the left edge again. Custom loading pictures of Battle.net servers (Eurobattle.net) are no longer cut up around the minimap.
- **1.15** — Warcraft III 1.28.5 support.
- **1.14** — Battle.net screens: the login doors reach the screen edges (no black areas beside them); the side panels of the chat, standard game, profile and team screens are widened like the other two-panel screens, with their contents; the top bar is extended to the screen edges with its own metalwork. `MenuLayout=0` now shows the menus as the original game does (stretched), with the 3D background unstretched. `FpsLimit`: the game's fullscreen refresh rate setting is raised to the monitor's highest rate when it would cap the frame rate (no more registry editing). The black area under the console now also works with Direct3D 8 wrappers and overlays. The time of day clock shows with `UIScale` above 100; multiboards and leaderboards are no longer pushed up with the world. The loading screen of DotA 6.8x maps is no longer stretched.
- **1.13** — `HideUIKey` (Ctrl+H by default): hides the whole interface in a game, including health bars and selection circles, for screenshots. New menu layout `MenuLayout=3`, the new default: as 2, but on the custom game screen the panels' contents stay centred in the widened panels. Clicks on the raised frames of the minimap and the command card no longer reach the world behind them.
- **1.12** — Clicks on the console's and top bar's artwork no longer reach the world behind them (with `WorldFullHeight=1` the world is also drawn there, and such a click selected or ordered units under the console); beside the console and the top bar the world still takes clicks. The frame around the minimap on the loading screen of melee maps now fits the map with `LoadingFullScreen=1` and `UIScale` (it was stretched into a rectangle, or larger than the map).
- **1.11** — Menu screens with two panels (custom game, LAN, saved games, custom campaigns, replays) no longer have a wide empty gap in the middle: the panels are widened towards the centre and meet there as on a 4:3 screen (`MenuLayout=2`, the new default; `MenuLayout=1` keeps the previous layout).
- **1.10.1** — Fixed the loading screen of melee maps (custom games on maps without their own loading screen): with `UIScale` its picture sat small in the lower left corner, and with `LoadingFullScreen=1` it was not stretched to the screen.
- **1.10** — Warcraft III 1.27b support. Fixed: after the first movie, the next movies in the same session were shown by the Windows renderer instead of the movie renderer.
- **1.9.1** — Fixed a crash when the sound provider is changed in the game's options (e.g. EAX switched on, with DSOAL).
- **1.9** — `ScoreScreen`: how the score screen fills a wide screen. 3 (new default): the sheet stretched to the width below its header, with the header cells and tabs in place. 2: the sheet extended to both edges, with its border on both sides (1.8.1). 1: original 4:3 with black bars.
- **1.8.1** — With `LoadingFullScreen=1` the score screen's sheet is extended to the screen edges instead of the bars being covered. With `UIScale` the score screen's parts stay in their places on its background.
- **1.8** — `UIScale`: size of the in-game interface in percent. `LoadingFullScreen`: loading screen picture over the whole screen. Movie subtitles (Subtitles on in the game's options) are shown with the movie renderer too, drawn by the renderer itself, so RTX HDR stays on; `MovieSubtitleSize` and `MovieSubtitleBrightness` set their size and brightness. With `LoadingFullScreen=1` the black bars beside the score screen are filled.
- **1.7.1** — On 4:3 screens the movie options (native mode, movie renderer, external player) and `FpsLimit` now work too; before, the mod did nothing there.
- **1.7** — DracoL1ch's DotA Allstars maps with the mod on (`DotAOriginal=0`, now the default): their interface is laid out over the wide screen, the 3D view is the one of the map's WideScreen option, over the full screen height, with a black backing under the console.
- **1.6** — `DotAOriginal`: DracoL1ch's DotA Allstars maps run as without the mod (they bring their own interface and WideScreen option); their loading screen picture is stretched to the screen width.
- **1.5.1** — The movie renderer (MPC Video Renderer, LAV Filters) is loaded at the first movie instead of at startup. Loaded at startup, it made DracoL1ch's DotA Allstars 7.0x maps crash at hero selection (heap corruption).
- **1.5** — `FpsLimit`: a frame rate limit above the engine's 64 fps, e.g. 144 for a 144 Hz monitor (0 = unchanged). Game logic, timers and animations stepped per tick are untouched; see "How it works".
- **1.4.1** — Map clicks now work in the strip beside the top bar. With `WorldFullHeight=1` the world was drawn up to the top edge, but the game still rejected clicks above the original top of the world view (an upper limit on the cursor's y before picking the terrain); that limit is now lifted when the world is drawn full height.
- **1.4** — First public release.
